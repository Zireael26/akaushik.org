09 · Experimental units
Sixteen tasks. Four families.
Paired candidate-minus-baseline outcomes are resampled 2,000 times with a fixed seed. Cluster resampling keeps each selected family together and weights by its tasks.
Only four families: this is a mechanics demonstration with weak inferential support. Three of the four family means coincide, so the family interval is narrower than the task interval and excludes zero; with four clusters there are only 256 distinct resamples, and that narrowness is an artifact, not stronger evidence. An interval is not the probability that the true value lies inside it.
