# Task contract

## Outcome

What authoritative state or artifact establishes completion? What must remain unchanged?

## Initial state and evidence

Which revision or snapshot applies? Which facts are known, unknown, or potentially stale? Where can each important claim be verified?

## Authority

Who requested the work? Which tenant, resources, tools, and effects are allowed? Which action requires a decision, and how will that decision bind to its exact payload?

## Execution bounds

What are the call, time, spend, concurrency, retry, and recursion limits? Who owns integration and cancellation?

## Verification

What checks distinguish the desired outcome from a plausible false success? How will prohibited effects, incomplete outcomes, and grader errors be identified?

## Recovery

What survives a restart? What can repeat? Which stable operation identity prevents duplicate effects under the backend's actual guarantees? What must be reconciled?

## Terminal statuses

Define verified success, needs information, denied, exhausted, cancelled, failed, and unknown pending reconciliation as applicable.
