## Interpretation

- The configured 400 KiB script warn ceiling still clears.
- The 150 KiB aspiration does not clear; current script transfer is about 292 KiB.
- Mobile performance clears the configured category threshold but misses the stricter LCP aspiration of <2.5s.
- The post-ADR-0012 raw-Three.js port remains the correct current state, but `three` itself is now the dominant browser cost.

## Follow-up path

1. Keep the 400 KiB CI ceiling as a warning-only guard until the site clears a lower measured budget.
2. Treat mobile LCP as the next user-facing performance target.
3. Decide in a future ADR whether the raw-Three.js hero is worth the remaining script cost or should move to a smaller WebGL/static implementation.
